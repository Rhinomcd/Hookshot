## Question

Pin the parts of the climb that charting left open:

- The noise threshold, from the tier-B harness's spread and tier A's own spread.
- Interleaved A/B runs versus back-to-back runs.
- The budget in rounds and tokens per round.
- What each member's brief contains: source, the numbers JSON, the incumbent's diff and the earlier rounds' results.
- The worktree and branch naming per candidate.
- The review score a kept diff must reach.
- How a tier-B failure on a tier-A winner is handled.
- Where each round's results are recorded so the PR body can carry the council table.
